# Golf Trip Mode — deferred, preserved scope

**Status: HOPPER ONLY. Do not implement or expose Trip Mode until the existing release-critical systems are complete and verified.**

Dagger Country Club's immediate ship criteria are:
1. Games and Ticket Window reliably use one score entry and correct accounting.
2. Money Room host QR permits easy, verified multi-phone joining.
3. Scalable, useful B/B+ pencil yardage books show actual available hole direction and trouble, without fabricated course facts.
4. House Ghost simulated shot-by-shot tracing is properly connected to live-round context, clearly distinguished from GPS tracking.
5. Approved R160 branding/seal preserved; only necessary usability fixes; verified staging then safe production release.

## Preserve for the later Golf Trip Mode milestone

- One shared trip, host-managed QR invitation and roster sized for **up to 64 golfers**, with multiple foursomes and teams.
- Multiple courses and rounds; individual, two-player, and team formats (including 2v2, 4v4, scrambles, alternate shot and rotating partners) subject to explicit game-rule validation.
- Simultaneous games (Nassau, Skins, team wagers and other supported games) feeding the **same** Trip Ledger; issue Ticket Window side bets against the trip.
- Live leaderboards, cumulative trip championship, and accurate final settlement, including correction/duplicate protection and exact-cent bookkeeping.
- A shareable **Clubhouse Report** after the trip: verified winners and losers, matchups/rivalries, Ghost results, funny trash talk and memorable moments.

Do not create a second money/accounting source. Build on the verified Money Room, scoring, Ledger, and course foundations after the current app is genuinely usable for a normal foursome.

Do not restart visual design, switch branding, or claim Trip Mode is released from this document.
